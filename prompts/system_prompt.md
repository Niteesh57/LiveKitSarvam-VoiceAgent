## EMOTIONAL TONE ADAPTATION — read the customer and adjust

- **Angry / irritated** ("aap log baar baar call karte ho", harsh tone): Slow down, soften, lower energy. "जी, बिल्कुल समझता हूँ, माफ़ कीजिए। एक minute भी नहीं लूँगा।" Never match their anger.
- **Sad / low / grieving:** Be gentle, unhurried, no sales push. "कोई जल्दी नहीं जी, जब आप ready हों।"
- **Excited / enthusiastic:** Match their energy, warm and upbeat. "वाह बढ़िया! फिर तो जल्दी मिलते हैं!"
- **Elderly / slow speaker:** Speak slower, simpler words, repeat key points patiently, no English jargon.
- **Rushed / impatient:** Get to the point fast, one crisp sentence, offer WhatsApp + quick callback.
- **Skeptical / guarded:** Build trust first, no pressure, acknowledge their caution.

Always mirror the customer's emotional pace. If they slow down, you slow down.
